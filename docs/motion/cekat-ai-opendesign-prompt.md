VISUAL DIRECTION
Global-standard product-first SaaS motion: precise hierarchy, generous whitespace, cinematic panel navigation, restrained 2.5D transitions, kinetic headline beats and continuous UI transformations. The video references inspire pacing, panel-to-panel camera movement, isolated feature closeups, exploded layers becoming one system, and a clean brand ending. Do not imitate their sneaker ads, Duolingo mascot, Anker screenshots, presenters, meme graphics or exaggerated marketing statistics.
Palette: paper #F9FAFB, white #FFFFFF, ink/navy #09214C, Cekat blue #1352BF, wordmark blue #005FCA, chat cyan #00B4C8, marketing orange #F99500, border #E5E9EF, secondary text #505F73. Use Google Sans Flex if available and legally packageable, otherwise Inter. Display headlines 76–104 px, supporting text 36–44 px, important UI 24–30 px, metadata 20–22 px at 1080p. Safe margins 96 px. Product windows radius 24 px, cards 16 px, compact chips 10 px, pill buttons. Soft shadows only under floating windows.
Camera perspective rotation at most 6 degrees, returning frontal for reading. One headline and one dominant action per scene. Easing: entrances cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), morphs cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1); 450–650 ms primary moves, 140–220 ms micro-feedback, 60–90 ms row staggers. Maya stays on WhatsApp; other inbox channels contain other people. Do not show autonomous fulfillment, predictive churn, automatic budget doubling, or AI access to stock without a connected source. Do not add Clara or MCP to this film.
Show “Illustrative demo” unobtrusively but legibly on all product scenes. Use fictional Aura Store, customer Maya, human agent Rafi. No #1, 10× productivity, guaranteed ROI, invented customer logos or revenue-uplift claims.

## Prompt revisi terarah

Gunakan satu revisi per pass setelah MP4 benar-benar ditinjau.

**Jika UI terlalu kecil:**

```text
Revise only scenes 04–09 (13–45s). Enlarge important UI text to at least 26px and reduce secondary rows rather than shrinking the full window. Keep the customer, campaign, order data, exact timings, palette, audio, other scenes and three-second closing hold unchanged. Render cekat-ai-connected-journey-v2.mp4. Preserve v1 and the editable source.
```

**Jika terlihat seperti slideshow:**

```text
Revise only the transitions between scenes 03–09. Carry the same source chip, Maya contact card and order record continuously between modules using shared-element transforms and state-preserving UI morphs. Replace generic dissolves, but keep the readable holds, exact 60-second timing, visible copy and all product truth constraints. Render a new version and preserve the previous MP4.
```

**Jika fulfillment terlihat otomatis:**

```text

Keep the order total IDR 300,000, other scenes, timing, audio and closing unchanged. Render a new MP4; do not just update the source preview.
```
