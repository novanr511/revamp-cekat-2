FAQ

Pricing questions

Want help choosing? Talk to our team ↗

Do your quotes include VAT?

No. Quotes from our sales team exclude VAT as required by local regulation, and you get the full breakdown before you subscribe.

What counts as an MAU (Monthly Active User)?

An MAU is a unique customer who interacts with your business in a given month. One customer counts once, no matter how many messages they send that month.

What is the difference between AI Simple and AI Full?

AI Simple answers questions from the knowledge base you provide. AI Full adds the ability to run more complex flows such as lead qualification, follow-ups, and taking action inside the chat.

Can I start with just one plan?

Yes. Many businesses start with Chat and add CRM or Marketing once the team settles in. Everything runs on the same platform, so your data connects the moment you add a product.

What if I need more than the largest plan?

Take the Custom plan. We size the capacity, seats, and integrations around your requirements, and confirm the commercial terms with you first.

Are there setup fees or minimum contracts?

Regular plans carry no separate setup fee and no minimum contract. For Custom, the commercial terms are agreed with our sales team before you begin.
